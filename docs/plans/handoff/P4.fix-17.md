I rule against that clause of the row; the planning session should ratify the deviation.
>
> WHY:
> - **Period:** every sentence of `describeOutcome` has none today ("Installed Serilog 3.0.0", "Restored 2 projects"), and the UI suites wait on prefixes. A period on the bulk forms alone would split one function's voice.
> - **Bulk of one:** UX-P4.fix-10 (DECISIONS row "One Restore path") names the subject at one ("Restored App") while the toast counts ("Restored 1 project."). Update All with one candidate is common, and "Updated Serilog 4.0.0" says more than "Updated 1 package". I follow fix-10, not the toast, here.
> - **No reason after a colon for several:** no bulk workflow in `PackageWorkflows.ts` sets `BulkOutcome.message` (every return at :119-180 is `{ opId, status, results, restores }`). Only the host's rejected builder (`handlers.ts:46`) and the queue's `derive` (`OperationQueue.ts:87`) do. So `outcomes.test.ts:49` ("1 of 2 operations completed: Moq: NU1101") pins a shape the product never emits. A bulk of one takes its reason from `results[0].message`.
> - **No "; n failed" clause:** fix-10's "Restored 1 of 2 projects" already drops it; "1 of 2" says it.
> - **Restore clause kept:** without it, "every edit done, one restore failed" (status `partial`, `feedback.test.ts:99`) would read as a success.
> - **Empty `results`:** the host falls back to `kind = 'restore'` (`feedback.ts:260`); mirroring that would say "Restored 0 projects" for an edit bulk, so empty results get neutral sentences.
>
> DO:
> 1. In `outcomes.ts`, extract the single branch unchanged into a local `describeSingle(outcome: OperationOutcome)`. Order the bulk branch as: rejected (unchanged), the restore branch (unchanged, `describeRestore`), empty, one, several.
> 2. Definitions: `total = results.length`; `done` = results with status `completed`; `kind = results[0].kind`; `id = results[0].packageId`; `failedRestores = restores.filter(r => !r.ok && !r.skipped).length` (the host's definition, `feedback.ts:259`).

>    | Status | Form | Examples |
>    |---|---|---|
>    | `completed` | `${DONE[kind]} ${what(total)}` | `Installed Serilog in 2 projects`, `Updated 2 packages`, `Uninstalled 2 packages` |

>    | `partial`, `failed` | `${DONE[kind]} ${what(done === total ? total : `${done} of ${total}`)}`, then `; 1 restore failed` or `; n restores failed` when `failedRestores > 0` | `Updated 1 of 2 packages`, `Uninstalled 0 of 2 packages`, `Installed Serilog in 1 of 2 projects`, `Updated 2 packages; 1 restore failed`, `Updated 1 of 2 packages; 1 restore failed` |
