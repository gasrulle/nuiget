>
> WHY:
> - **Loaded and empty already read 20.** `.nuiget-details-info` and `.nuiget-readme` pad `--nuiget-inset-details` (`src/webview/main/main.css:258-268`), and the empty state reads `empty top 20 (target 20)` under DS-INSET-4 and DS-STATE-3 ("20 in the details pane").
