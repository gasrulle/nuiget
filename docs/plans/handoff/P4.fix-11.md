### 2026-11-18, UX-P4.fix-11, design-advisor opening ruling

Question: the sentence form, the status-bar line, who speaks when the origin surface cannot, bulk menu outcomes, cancelled and rejected.

> DECISION: A row-menu operation is the invoking surface's own operation in every respect: (a) `describeOutcome` unchanged; (d) the status-bar line goes when the outcome carries an origin and stays only for a host-submitted single success without one; (e) no fallback speaker; (g) a bulk menu outcome is said in the live region as well as by its toast; `cancelled` and `rejected` use the existing sentences. No fallback needs the host to know whether a surface received the outcome.
>
> WHY:
> - **Parity is the rule.** A button-started operation is said once by the surface that asked, in `describeOutcome`'s words, with no status-bar outcome line (DECISIONS "One webview announcer per outcome", DS-STATE-5, D50). The menu is another way to ask from the same row, so it gets the same report.
> - **Status bar.** My UX-P4.7 ruling gave the line as the visible stand-in while no webview could speak, and called this row "the announcer". Keeping both gives two reports of one success, which is X40's defect (`docs/research/2026-10-23-ux-research.md:599`). The workbench exemplar is the one DS-STATE-5 cites: an Extensions install announces through `alert(…)` and writes no status-bar message.
> - **Fallback.** A button-started operation whose panel is closed before the outcome has no announcer and no status line today; the menu case now matches it.
